# Credits and Acknowledgments

Brave Music is built with the help of foundational open-source technologies, libraries, and communities. We express our gratitude to the creators and maintainers of these projects:

---

## 1. Governance and Leadership

- **Codemo Open Source Labs (Codemo OSL)**: Product architecture, project governance, design direction, and release maintenance. Visit [codemoteams.org](https://codemoteams.org) for official programs and community initiatives.

---

## 2. Core Upstream Technologies

- **CastLabs**: For maintaining custom Electron releases bundled with Widevine CDM support, enabling lawful and smooth DRM audio streaming on Linux platforms.
- **Musikcube**: Authored by Casey Langen and open-source contributors. The lightweight C++ terminal and background audio engine that powers local library indexing, decoding, and streaming in Brave Music.
- **Brave Software**: For pioneering privacy-respecting ad and tracker blocking architectures that inspired the Brave Shields filtering system implemented in our client.
- **yt-dlp Team**: For maintaining the robust media extraction utility used by our background download service to save offline audio tracks.
- **Chromium Project**: For the underlying browser engine and Web Audio implementations.

---

## 3. Community Contributors

Thank you to all developers, bug reporters, and community members participating in discussions on our Discord server and submitting pull requests. Active contributors will be recognized in future release notes and community halls of fame.
