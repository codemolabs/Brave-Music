Dedicated Browser Protocol (CDP)

Authentic YouTube Music Session Synchronization

YouTube Music uses authentic session synchronization. We launch a dedicated, isolated external browser window via Chrome DevTools Protocol (CDP) so you can sign into Google normally without embedded app blocks. Once authenticated, cookies (__Secure-3PAPISID, LOGIN_INFO) are automatically captured and injected into your player.
